# Small model results

Generated by `scripts/slm_results.py` from `runs/` and `results/train_logs/`. Every small-model run here is scored in bf16 on the full eval set. `E1000` is the first 1,000 L2DTnH test messages, scored against the expert annotators (102 toxic). The pressure set is 40 hard lines, 4 for each of 10 kinds, labelled by the author under [docs/LABELING_RULES.md](../docs/LABELING_RULES.md) (11 toxic, 29 not). Bar, set before any run: precision ≥ 0.80 and F1 ≥ 0.75 on `E1000`. A reply counts only if it is exactly `toxic` or `nontoxic`; anything else is scored wrong.

† The L2DTnH authors' own fine-tune of `unitary/toxic-bert` (Ave et al., ESORICS 2026), pinned to the data's commit and scored by `src/run_paper_bert.py` in fp32 on the CPU, with the paper's threshold (0.58) and 192-token limit. Its training script used the test split as its validation set: it kept the epoch with the lowest test loss and chose the threshold on the test split. `E1000` is the first 1,000 rows of that split, so its `E1000` scores are flattered. At the default 0.50 threshold it scores precision 0.785 and F1 0.749 on `E1000`; the epoch choice can't be undone without retraining. Its training never saw the pressure set, so the pressure column is the fair comparison.
