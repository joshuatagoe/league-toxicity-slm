How experienced players labelled this chat:
- Toxic: insults, harassment, an offensive tone, or otherwise hostile content aimed at players, such as name-calling, telling someone to shut up, blaming a player, profanity aimed at someone, slurs, and taunting.
- Sarcasm or playful teasing counts as toxic only if a player would reasonably take it as offensive in a competitive match.
- Neutral game talk is not toxic: callouts, strategy, questions, praise, greetings, laughter and short replies.
- Judge only the words of the message. A message counts as toxic if even a couple of experienced players would find it offensive.

Check, in this order:
1. Is the message aimed at a player, the team or the enemy team, or only at the game, a champion or the speaker?
2. Does it insult, blame, mock, taunt or swear at that target, directly or through sarcasm?
3. Would a couple of experienced players reasonably find it offensive in a competitive match?
