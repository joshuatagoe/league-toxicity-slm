# Labelling rules (draft)

Drafted 2026-10-29 from the author's relabelling decisions and answers. These rules are the basis for the project's behaviour spec. Edit them where they don't match your judgement.

## The question

Would an experienced League player reasonably find this line offensive to the player it's aimed at? Judge the line, not the player, and not whether anyone deserves punishment. Use the surrounding chat to understand what the line means: who it's aimed at, whether it's sarcastic, whether it finishes an insult split across messages.

**When the answer depends on context we don't have** (the game state, an earlier provocation that isn't shown), **label it not toxic.**

## Toxic

| Rule | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Insults or name-calling aimed at a player | `noob kata you feed`, `jungle you're actually useless` |
| Disguised insults: odd spelling or polite put-downs | `u r tr4sh`, `n00b team`, `go play some other game pls`, `have u considered tft` |
| Sarcasm that mocks a teammate | `wow great gank jungle, really helpful`, `0/6 at 12 min, truly a genius mid`, `ty for the kill steal adc <3` |
| Taunting the enemy | `gg ez`, `ez lane, go next`, `1v1 me noob` |
| "diff" put-downs | `jg diff` |
| Insults in another language, when you know the meaning | `grosse pute` |

## Not toxic

| Rule | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Feeding talk on its own: information, not an attack | `soraka feed`, `who fed kayle`, `that rengar so fed` |
| Plain report calls, or quoting or reporting someone else's insult | `report mid and bot`, `report our jungle, he called our adc a retard` |
| Self-criticism | `im so bad this game sorry guys`, `i should uninstall after that flash lol` |
| Talk about champions, items or the game, including swearing at it | `yasuo is such a useless champ this patch`, `fucking lag again` |
| Blunt callouts and blame without an insult | `stop pushing, jungle is top`, `why did you go in alone` |
| Friendly banter | `haha nice try lee, almost had me`, `lmao that flash into wall` |
| Telling someone who is being toxic to stop, even angrily | `stop flame and help`, `stop fucking being toxic` (as a reply to harassment) |
| Surrender calls without an insult | `surr 20`, `ff, bot is feeding` |
| Lines that depend on unseen context | `nice ward supp, very useful`, `cry more top` |

Adding an insult, swearing at the player, mockery or a slur to any of these makes it toxic: `noob soraka feed`, `surrender noobs`.

## Where these differ from the L2DTnH experts

- The experts labelled many bare feed lines toxic (`soraka feed`), inconsistently.
- For the first 5,000 messages, the experts' rule made a line toxic if just 2 of 8 annotators flagged it, so borderline lines lean toxic; the rest were settled by 3 annotators in discussion. These rules lean not toxic when context is missing.
- The experts labelled most plain report calls fine, as these rules do.
