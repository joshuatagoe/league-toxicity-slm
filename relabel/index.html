League Chat Relabel Desk

What this is

This is a Gauntlet Week 7 project: a small AI model that spots toxic chat in League of Legends. Its training data comes from a published research dataset where expert players labelled real chat from reported games. Those labels are disputed, so a few experienced players are labelling the same lines independently. Your labels show where real players agree, and where the experts or the AI get it wrong.

About 20–25 minutes. Part A has 100 real messages, each shown inside the chat around it. Part B has 40 short lines with no chat around them. You see no one else's labels, and no one else sees yours.

How to judge each line

Would an experienced League player reasonably find this highlighted line offensive to the player it's aimed at?

  • Toxic means insults, harassment, an offensive tone, or other hostile content aimed at players. Sarcasm or teasing counts only if a player would reasonably take it as offensive in a competitive match.
  • Judge the line, not the player. This isn't a punishment call: don't ask whether someone deserves a ban.
  • Use the chat around it to understand the line: who it's aimed at, whether it's sarcastic, whether it finishes an insult typed across several messages. Don't mark a harmless line toxic just because the same player flamed earlier.
  • Pick Unsure when the answer honestly depends on something you can't see, such as the score or what happened in the game.
  • Go with your own judgement as a player. There's no answer key to match. Disagreement between players is exactly what this measures.
